What is Online BCom?

Bachelor of Commerce (Online BCom) is a UGC-DEB approved degree/diploma you can complete online, with recorded and live classes, online assignments and proctored or centre-based exams. It is designed for working professionals and students who need flexibility without relocating.

Online BCom Eligibility

  • 10+2 (any stream) from a recognised board
  • Minimum marks vary by university (commonly 40-50%)
  • No entrance exam in most online programmes

Admission Process

  1. Pick your programme and shortlist 2-3 UGC-DEB approved universities
  2. Compare fees, NAAC grade, exam mode and placement support
  3. Fill the online application on the university portal and upload documents
  4. Pay the application / first-semester fee (many universities offer 0% EMI)
  5. Receive your confirmation and LMS login, then start classes

Online BCom FAQs

Is Online BCom valid in India?

Yes, when the university and the programme are UGC-DEB approved for your admission session. Always verify on our UGC-DEB checker before paying any fee.

What is the duration of Online BCom?

Usually 3 years (6 semesters), depending on the university and mode of study.

What is the fee of Online BCom?

Roughly ₹30,000 – ₹1,20,000 for the full programme, depending on the university and specialisation. Check each university page for current fees.

Can I prepare for CA while doing online BCom?

Yes, many students do. The flexible schedule helps, but plan your CA coaching hours separately.
