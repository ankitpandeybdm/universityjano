Frequently asked questions

Is the Online MBA from Manipal University Jaipur valid?

Manipal University Jaipur is NAAC A+ accredited (3.28 CGPA) and offers UGC-entitled online programmes. Always confirm the programme and your admission session on the UGC-DEB portal before paying.

What is the fee of the Online MBA at Manipal University Jaipur?

The official total fee is ₹1,80,000 for the 24-month programme, shown here without any discount or scholarship. A registration fee of ₹500 applies for Indian students as per the fee sheet. Connect with a counsellor to check which discounts you may qualify for.

What is the eligibility for the Online MBA?

Graduation with 50% marks (45% for reserved categories). Please confirm the latest criteria with a counsellor or the university before applying.

How long is the programme and how are exams conducted?

The Online MBA runs for 24 months (4 semesters). Classes are live and recorded, and exams are online proctored and can be taken from home.

Which specialisations can I choose?

There are 13 electives: Finance, Marketing, HRM, Analytics & Data Science, Retail Management, BFSI, IT & FinTech, Operations Management, International Business, Information Systems Management, Supply Chain Management, Project Management and Total Quality Management.

Are there placements after the Online MBA?

Online Manipal offers free placement assistance to eligible learners and reports 500+ hiring partners, 2,500+ offers and an average salary of about 6 LPA (depending on work experience and industry) across Online Manipal overall. This is assistance, not a job guarantee.

Is there an EMI or loan option?

The fee sheet lists no-cost EMI and loan options through finance partners. Ask a counsellor for the current partners and terms.
