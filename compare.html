A side-by-side comparison of fees, NAAC grades, 0% EMI options, placement support, and exam mode across UGC-DEB approved online universities in India — so you can shortlist with real data instead of marketing claims.

Choosing an online degree usually comes down to a handful of factors that matter more than a university's brand name: whether the specific programme is UGC-DEB approved for your admission session, the real fee structure after any discounts, placement support for working professionals, and whether exams are proctored online or held at a physical centre. This tool lines up that information across universities so you can compare them directly.
