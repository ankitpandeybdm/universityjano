How to Compare Online Universities in 5 Steps

  1. Check approval. Confirm UGC-DEB entitlement for your programme and session with our UGC Verifier.
  2. Compare total fees. Look at the full programme fee, not only the semester fee, and test EMI in our EMI calculator.
