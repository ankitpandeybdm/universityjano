Frequently Asked Questions

What is UGC-DEB approval and why does it matter for an online degree?

UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau) approval is what makes an online degree from an Indian university legally equivalent to a regular on-campus degree for jobs, higher education and most government eligibility. Always verify current-session approval for your exact program before paying any fee, using our free checker.
