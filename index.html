Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers based on official UGC guidelines and statutory consumer education regulations.

What is UGC-DEB approval and why does it matter for an online degree? expand_more
UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau) approval is what makes an online degree from an Indian university legally equivalent to a regular on-campus degree for jobs, higher education, and government exams (UPSC, SSC, PSU, Banking). Always verify current-session approval for your exact program before paying any fee.
How much does an online MBA cost in India in 2025-2026? expand_more
A UGC-DEB approved online MBA typically costs between ₹50,000 and ₹2,50,000 for the full two-year program, depending on the university tier (NAAC A++, A+) and specialization, with 0% EMI options starting as low as ₹4,500/month at most approved universities.
Will 'Online Mode' be written on my official degree certificate? expand_more
As per UGC regulations, the degree certificate mentions the program name (e.g., 'Master of Business Administration') without stigmatizing differences, adhering strictly to UGC (ODL & Online Programmes) Regulations 2020 which establishes equal statutory parity with on-campus awards.
Is UniversityJano's counseling and comparison service really 100% free? expand_more
Yes — comparing universities, checking current UGC-DEB statutory approvals, running fee and ROI calculators, and speaking 1-on-1 with senior academic counselors on UniversityJano.com is 100% free for students with zero hidden platform charges.
Do top IT and Fortune 500 corporate employers accept online degrees in India? expand_more
Yes. Leading companies including Google, Amazon, Microsoft, TCS, Infosys, Deloitte, and HDFC actively accept UGC-DEB approved degrees for promotions, lateral hiring, and salary increments when backed by relevant technical skills and practical experience.
