Don't Mix These Up

UGC-DEB, NAAC and NIRF: Three Terms, Three Different Jobs

Every university page throws all three at you. Only one of them decides whether your online degree is valid.

The one that matters most

UGC-DEB

The Distance Education Bureau of the UGC says whether a university may offer a specific programme in online or distance mode for a given session. If this is missing, nothing else on the brochure rescues the degree.

Ask:Is this programme approved for this session?
Quality grade

NAAC

A grade (A++, A+, A…) and a score out of 4 for overall quality. UGC's online-education rules use a minimum NAAC score — or a top-100 NIRF rank — as a threshold for starting online programmes, and institutions just below it can apply for approval. Those rules have been amended since 2020, so confirm the current criteria on the DEB portal.

Ask:Is the grade current, and is the score listed?
A ranking, not a licence

NIRF

A government ranking of institutions. It's a helpful signal, but a rank is not an approval, and it describes the university overall — not its online programme specifically.

Ask:Which category and year is this rank from?

Rule of thumb: approval first, grade second, rank last. You can check any university on our UGC-DEB verifier, and see all three numbers side by side for up to three universities in Compare.

Protect Your Money

How to Spot a Fake University Before You Pay

UGC publishes a list of fake universities every few months. Scammers don't wait for it — these are the patterns to watch for.

1
The name is almost a real oneOne word added, one word changed. Always match the exact name against UGC's list, not the brochure's spelling.
2
Fees go to a personal accountGenuine universities collect fees in an institutional account. Be wary of a personal UPI ID or a "send it to our agent" request.
3
"Degree in 6 months" promisesSkipped years, "direct second-year entry", or credit for work experience in place of study — recognised degrees don't work this way.
4
A generic "UGC Approved" badgeApproval is per programme and per session. A logo with no programme name or session is not proof.
5
Pressure to pay today"Seats closing tonight" or a discount that vanishes at midnight. Real admission windows are published and can be checked.
6
A website you only found via WhatsAppOpen the university's site from its listing on UGC's page, not from a link someone forwarded.

Quick Answers

Questions Students Ask Us Most

What is UGC-DEB approval and why does it matter for an online degree?

UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau) approval is what makes an online degree from an Indian university legally equivalent to a regular on-campus degree for jobs, higher education and most government eligibility. Always verify current-session approval for your exact program before paying any fee.

How much does an online MBA cost in India in 2026?

A UGC-DEB approved online MBA in India typically costs between ₹50,000 and ₹2,50,000 for the full program, depending on the university and specialization, with 0% EMI options available at most approved universities.

Is UniversityJano's counselling and comparison service free?

Yes. Comparing universities, checking UGC-DEB approval, using the fee and ROI calculators, and getting 1-on-1 expert counselling on UniversityJano.com are all completely free for students.

Do employers in India accept online degrees?

Yes, most Indian employers now accept UGC-DEB approved online degrees at par with regular degrees, since they carry the same legal recognition. Employer perception can still vary by industry and individual company.

What is the difference between online, distance and regular degrees?

Regular means classroom study on campus. Online means you study through a learning portal with live and recorded lectures, and most exams are proctored from home. Distance (ODL) is self-paced study with printed or digital material, and exams are often held at study centres. Approved online and distance degrees carry the same standing as regular ones under UGC regulations, but check the notification of any specific job or exam you are aiming for.

How do I check whether a university is fake?

Match the exact university name against UGC's list of fake universities, confirm that your programme is approved for online or distance mode in your admission session on the UGC-DEB portal, and pay fees only to the university's official account.
