Frequently Asked Questions: Career ROI & Degree Legality

Answers to the most critical employer acceptance and regulatory questions.

Do top MNCs and corporates accept online degrees at par with regular on-campus degrees?

expand_more
Yes. As per the official University Grants Commission (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020 (Gazette Notification Section 22), degrees acquired through UGC-DEB entitled online and ODL modes from recognized universities are treated at par with degrees acquired through conventional regular face-to-face higher education modes for both corporate hiring and central/state government examinations.

How does UniversityJano compute the payback period and 5-year ROI?

expand_more
Our algorithm measures the net difference between your post-degree starting package and your baseline salary trajectory. The payback period formula is: Total Course Fee ÷ (Post-Degree Monthly In-hand Salary – Pre-Degree Monthly In-hand Salary). The 5-year compounding wealth metric assumes standard lateral transitions at Year 3 and leadership appraisals at Year 5 based on verified alumni benchmark metrics.

Does the final degree certificate say "Online Mode" or look different?

expand_more
UGC regulations state that the degree certificate confers the recognized title (e.g. Master of Business Administration, Master of Computer Applications). Some universities indicate the mode in accordance with UGC guidelines, but statutory equivalence remains equal. Employers focus primarily on your portfolio, practical execution skills, and institutional accreditation (NAAC A++, NIRF ranking).

Can I finance my online degree through 0% interest monthly EMI?

expand_more
Yes. Most premier partner universities (Manipal, Amity, Jain, NMIMS, LPU) offer direct 0% interest monthly installments across 10 to 24 months through NBFC education financing partners (such as GrayQuest, Propelld, LiquiLoans). You can start an online degree with monthly payments as low as ₹4,999 without any upfront collateral.
