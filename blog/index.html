About This Blog

UniversityJano.com helps Indian students and working professionals find and join UGC-DEB approved online degree programs — Online MBA, MCA, BBA, BCA and Data Science — without the usual confusion around fees, approvals, and admissions agents. Every guide on this blog is written to answer the exact questions students ask us during free counselling calls: is this degree valid, what does it really cost after EMI, and will employers accept it.

Before you shortlist a program, two tools on this site are worth using alongside these guides: the free UGC-DEB Approval Checker to confirm a university's current-session entitlement, and the university comparison tool to see real fees, NAAC grades and placement data side by side across 25+ approved universities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are online degrees from UGC-DEB approved universities valid in India?

Yes. Degrees from universities approved by the UGC's Distance Education Bureau (DEB) carry the same legal validity as regular on-campus degrees for employment, higher education and most government job eligibility, provided the specific program and academic session were approved at the time you enrolled.

How do I check if a university is UGC-DEB approved?

Use our free UGC-DEB Approval Checker to confirm current-session approval before paying any fee — approval status can change year to year, so always verify for the exact session you're enrolling in.

Is 0% EMI really interest-free for online degree fees?

Genuine no-cost EMI plans split your total fee across months with no added interest, though eligibility depends on your credit profile and the partner lender. Use our EMI calculator to see exact monthly numbers before committing.
