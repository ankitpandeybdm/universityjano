UGC-DEB Verification Tool

UGC-DEB Government Approval & Legal Validity Inspector

Check whether a university's online or distance-mode programme is entitled by the UGC Distance Education Bureau (DEB) for your exact course and admission session — the single approval that decides if your degree is valid for government jobs, UPSC, UGC NET, and further studies.

An online or distance degree is legally equivalent to a regular, on-campus degree only when the offering university holds valid UGC-DEB entitlement for that specific programme and academic session. Approval is granted programme-by-programme and session-by-session, not once for the whole university — so verifying it before you enrol, and before you pay any fee, is the single most important check a student can make.

What This Tool Checks

  • Whether your chosen university is UGC-DEB entitled to offer Online or ODL programmes.
  • Whether that entitlement covers your specific programme (e.g. Online MBA, Online MCA, Online BBA).
  • Whether the entitlement is valid for your intended academic session.
  • Additional recognitions worth checking — NAAC grade, AICTE approval where applicable, and NCTE recognition for teacher-education programmes.
