Frequently Asked Questions on UGC-DEB Norms

Statutory answers cited from the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations.

What is the difference between UGC Approved and UGC-DEB Entitled? expand_more

"UGC Approved" designates that an institution is a recognized university under Section 2(f) or 3 of the UGC Act 1956. However, to deliver programs in online or distance learning modes, the university MUST hold explicit program-by-program entitlement issued by the Distance Education Bureau (DEB). A university can be UGC approved for on-campus education while having zero legal validity for online programs.

Will my degree certificate state the word "Online" on it? expand_more

As mandated by UGC Regulation 15, the degree or diploma awarded through online mode shall explicitly mention "Online Mode" on the reverse or header of the certificate. Importantly, the UGC has issued statutory directives affirming that such degrees carry 100% equal parity with conventional degrees for higher education admissions and government recruitment.

Are Online Technical and Professional Degrees valid (e.g. MBA, MCA)? expand_more

Yes. Online MBA and Online MCA degrees are legally recognized provided the university holds joint approval from the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and entitlement from UGC-DEB. Programs in Engineering (B.Tech/M.Tech), Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law, and Agriculture are strictly prohibited under UGC regulations from being offered fully online.

Can I prepare for UPSC Civil Services or PSU exams with an Online Degree? expand_more

Yes, without restriction. The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Staff Selection Commission (SSC), and State PSCs accept degrees awarded by any recognized university established under an Act of Parliament or State Legislature. Since UGC-DEB recognized online degrees are legally equivalent to on-campus degrees under Central Gazette law, graduates are completely eligible to sit for all government exams.

How often does the UGC update the list of entitled universities? expand_more

The UGC updates its entitlement list biannually, aligning with the July/August (Monsoon) and January/February (Winter) academic admission sessions.
