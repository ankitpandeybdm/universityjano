🎓 What Is an Online Degree?

An online degree is a full academic degree — undergraduate or postgraduate — delivered entirely through digital learning platforms rather than on a physical campus. In India, this is distinct from older-style "distance education" correspondence courses: modern online degrees include live classes, recorded lectures, digital assignments, and proctored online examinations, and are governed by specific UGC regulations rather than being treated as a lesser alternative to campus study.

✅ Is It Valid? UGC-DEB Approval Explained

Every legitimate online degree in India is offered under the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020. A university's Distance Education Bureau (DEB) approval must cover the specific program and the specific academic session you enroll in — approval is not a permanent, blanket stamp for the whole university. This is the single most important thing to verify before paying any fee.

💼 Work Integrated Degree Programs

If you're a working professional looking to deepen expertise in your current field rather than switch industries, a work integrated degree program (WILP) ties coursework directly to real workplace problems — often with employer involvement or sponsorship. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are online degrees valid in India?

Yes, degrees from UGC-DEB approved universities carry the same legal validity as on-campus degrees for employment, government jobs, and higher education, provided the program and session were approved.
