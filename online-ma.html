What is Online MA?

Master of Arts (Online MA) is a UGC-DEB approved degree/diploma you can complete online, with recorded and live classes, online assignments and proctored or centre-based exams. It is designed for working professionals and students who need flexibility without relocating.

Online MA Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognised university (subject rules vary)
  • Minimum marks vary by university (commonly 45-50%)
  • No entrance exam in most online programmes

Admission Process

  1. Pick your programme and shortlist 2-3 UGC-DEB approved universities
  2. Compare fees, NAAC grade, exam mode and placement support
  3. Fill the online application on the university portal and upload documents
  4. Pay the application / first-semester fee (many universities offer 0% EMI)
  5. Receive your confirmation and LMS login, then start classes

Online MA FAQs

Is Online MA valid in India?

Yes, when the university and the programme are UGC-DEB approved for your admission session. Always verify on our UGC-DEB checker before paying any fee.

What is the duration of Online MA?

Usually 2 years (4 semesters), depending on the university and mode of study.

What is the fee of Online MA?

Roughly ₹20,000 – ₹70,000 for the full programme, depending on the university and specialisation. Check each university page for current fees.

Which MA subject is best for UPSC preparation?

Many aspirants pick Public Administration, Sociology, Political Science or History because of syllabus overlap; choose the subject you can score in.
