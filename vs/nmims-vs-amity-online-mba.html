Fees: NMIMS Online vs Amity Online

NMIMS Online lists the Online MBA at ₹1,96,000 (₹49,000 per semester). Amity Online lists ₹1,79,000 (₹44,750 per semester). Amity Online is listed ₹17,000 (about 9%) cheaper in total.

On the 0% EMI plans as listed, the monthly amount is about ₹4,900 for NMIMS Online and ₹4,475 for Amity Online (a difference of about ₹425 a month). Work out what suits your budget with our EMI calculator and check the payback with the ROI and salary calculator.

Who Should Choose Which?

Choose NMIMS Online if…

  • You want the stronger institutional credentials: NAAC A++ (3.67 CGPA) against A+ (3.27 CGPA), and a NIRF position listed as "NIRF Top 21 University in India".
  • You prefer case-study-led learning (NMIMS Student Portal & Mobile App with Case Studies from Harvard & Ivey) and computer-based exams at home or at an exam centre.
  • You meet the 50% marks requirement and are comfortable paying ₹17,000 more.

Choose Amity Online if…

  • Your graduation marks are below 50%: Amity Online lists a 40% minimum in any stream.
  • You want to save ₹17,000 on the total fee (₹1,79,000 against ₹1,96,000).
  • You want 1-on-1 mentorship on Amity's own LMS and value its large alumni base.

Our take: The fee gap is only about 9%, while NMIMS lists the higher NAAC grade and reports higher CTC figures (as stated by the universities). If you meet the 50% marks requirement, NMIMS looks stronger on listed credentials. Amity is the route if your marks are lower or you want to keep the fee down.

Placements: What the Universities Report

NMIMS Online reports a highest CTC of 22 LPA and an average of 9.2 LPA for the MBA. Amity Online reports 18.5 LPA highest and 7.9 LPA average. These are university-reported figures and not a job guarantee. Outcomes for online learners depend heavily on prior experience, specialization and effort.

NMIMS Online vs Amity Online MBA FAQs

Which online MBA is cheaper, NMIMS Online or Amity Online?

Amity Online is listed ₹17,000 (about 9%) cheaper in total. The listed total fees are ₹1,96,000 for NMIMS Online and ₹1,79,000 for Amity Online. Fees change by session and payment plan, so confirm the current figure with the university before paying.

Which has the higher NAAC grade, NMIMS Online or Amity Online?

NMIMS Online is listed at the higher NAAC grade (NMIMS Online: A++, 3.67 CGPA; Amity Online: A+, 3.27 CGPA). NAAC grades the whole university, not the online MBA on its own, so use it as one input among several.

Are NMIMS Online and Amity Online UGC-DEB approved?

Both are listed as UGC-DEB approved on UniversityJano for 2026-27. Approval is programme-wise and session-wise, so verify the exact MBA programme on the UGC-DEB portal (deb.ugc.ac.in) before admission.

What is the eligibility for the NMIMS Online and Amity Online online MBA?

NMIMS Online: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with min 50% marks. Amity Online: Graduation in any stream with min 40% marks.

Which has more MBA specializations, NMIMS Online or Amity Online?

Both list 6 specializations. The full lists are in the comparison table above.
