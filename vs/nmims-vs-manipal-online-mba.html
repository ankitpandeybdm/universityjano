Fees: NMIMS Online vs Manipal Online

NMIMS Online lists the Online MBA at ₹1,96,000 (₹49,000 per semester). Manipal Online lists ₹1,75,000 (₹43,750 per semester). Manipal Online is listed ₹21,000 (about 11%) cheaper in total.

On the 0% EMI plans as listed, the monthly amount is about ₹4,900 for NMIMS Online and ₹4,375 for Manipal Online (a difference of about ₹525 a month). Work out what suits your budget with our EMI calculator and check the payback with the ROI and salary calculator.

Who Should Choose Which?

Choose NMIMS Online if…

  • You want the stronger institutional credentials: NAAC A++ (3.67 CGPA) and a NIRF position listed as "NIRF Top 21 University in India".
  • You are comfortable paying a premium: the listed MBA fee is ₹1,96,000, which is ₹21,000 more than Manipal Online.
  • You prefer computer-based exams that can be taken at home or at an exam centre.

Choose Manipal Online if…

  • You want to keep the total fee lower (₹1,75,000 against ₹1,96,000).
  • You want a wider specialization menu (10 against 6), including FinTech, Supply Chain and International Business.
  • You like the live weekend masterclass format with Coursera access.

Our take: NMIMS lists the higher NAAC score and NIRF position and charges about ₹21,000 more. Manipal gives more specializations at the lower fee. If the institutional name matters most to you, lean NMIMS; if flexibility and budget matter most, lean Manipal.

Placements: What the Universities Report

NMIMS Online vs Manipal Online MBA FAQs

Which online MBA is cheaper, NMIMS Online or Manipal Online?

Manipal Online is listed ₹21,000 (about 11%) cheaper in total. The listed total fees are ₹1,96,000 for NMIMS Online and ₹1,75,000 for Manipal Online. Fees change by session and payment plan, so confirm the current figure with the university before paying.

Which has the higher NAAC grade, NMIMS Online or Manipal Online?

NMIMS Online is listed at the higher NAAC grade (NMIMS Online: A++, 3.67 CGPA; Manipal Online: A+, 3.28 CGPA). NAAC grades the whole university, not the online MBA on its own, so use it as one input among several.

Are NMIMS Online and Manipal Online UGC-DEB approved?

Both are listed as UGC-DEB approved on UniversityJano for 2026-27. Approval is programme-wise and session-wise, so verify the exact MBA programme on the UGC-DEB portal (deb.ugc.ac.in) before admission.
