Fees: Amity Online vs Jain Online

Amity Online lists the Online MBA at ₹1,79,000 (₹44,750 per semester). Jain Online lists ₹1,60,000 (₹40,000 per semester). Jain Online is listed ₹19,000 (about 11%) cheaper in total.

On the 0% EMI plans as listed, the monthly amount is about ₹4,475 for Amity Online and ₹4,000 for Jain Online (a difference of about ₹475 a month). Work out what suits your budget with our EMI calculator and check the payback with the ROI and salary calculator.

Who Should Choose Which?

Choose Amity Online if…

  • You need a lower marks cut-off: Amity Online lists a minimum of 40% in any stream, against 50% (45% reserved) at Jain Online.
  • You like 1-on-1 mentorship and the Amigo LMS experience (Amigo LMS Platform with Live Masterclasses & 1-on-1 Mentorship).
  • Amity's brand recognition and large alumni base matter more to you than the listed fee difference.

Choose Jain Online if…

  • You want to pay less: the listed MBA fee is ₹1,60,000, which is ₹19,000 below Amity Online.
  • You want the higher NAAC grade: A++ (3.71 CGPA (Highest Category)) against A+ (3.27 CGPA).
  • You want links with Bangalore's IT and start-up hiring ecosystem, which Jain highlights, and can set aside regular weekly time for projects.

Our take: Jain lists the lower fee (₹19,000 less) and the higher NAAC grade, and both offer six specializations. Amity is the easier route on eligibility. For most working professionals who meet the 50% mark, Jain looks better on value, while Amity suits those below that mark.

Placements: What the Universities Report

Amity Online reports a highest CTC of 18.5 LPA and an average of 7.9 LPA for the MBA. Jain Online reports 21.5 LPA highest and 8.6 LPA average. These are university-reported figures and not a job guarantee. Outcomes for online learners depend heavily on prior experience, specialization and effort.

Amity Online vs Jain Online MBA FAQs

Which online MBA is cheaper, Amity Online or Jain Online?

Jain Online is listed ₹19,000 (about 11%) cheaper in total. The listed total fees are ₹1,79,000 for Amity Online and ₹1,60,000 for Jain Online. Fees change by session and payment plan, so confirm the current figure with the university before paying.

Which has the higher NAAC grade, Amity Online or Jain Online?

Jain Online is listed at the higher NAAC grade (Amity Online: A+, 3.27 CGPA; Jain Online: A++, 3.71 CGPA (Highest Category)). NAAC grades the whole university, not the online MBA on its own, so use it as one input among several.

Are Amity Online and Jain Online UGC-DEB approved?

Both are listed as UGC-DEB approved on UniversityJano for 2026-27. Approval is programme-wise and session-wise, so verify the exact MBA programme on the UGC-DEB portal (deb.ugc.ac.in) before admission.

What is the eligibility for the Amity Online and Jain Online online MBA?

Amity Online: Graduation in any stream with min 40% marks. Jain Online: Graduation with min 50% marks (45% for reserved).
