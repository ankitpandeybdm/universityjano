Symbiosis Online lists the Online MBA at ₹1,40,000 (₹35,000 per semester). Amity Online lists ₹1,79,000 (₹44,750 per semester). Symbiosis Online is listed ₹39,000 (about 22%) cheaper in total.

On the 0% EMI plans as listed, the monthly amount is about ₹3,500 for Symbiosis Online and ₹4,475 for Amity Online (a difference of about ₹975 a month). Work out what suits your budget with our EMI calculator and check the payback with the ROI and salary calculator.

Who Should Choose Which?

Choose Symbiosis Online if…

  • You want to pay less: Symbiosis Online lists ₹1,40,000, which is ₹39,000 (about 22%) below Amity Online.
  • You want the higher NAAC grade as listed: A++ (3.58 CGPA) against A+ (3.27 CGPA).
  • You want Operations or Banking as a specialization, which Amity Online does not list, plus on-demand computer-based exam slots.

Choose Amity Online if…

  • You want 1-on-1 mentorship and live masterclasses on Amity's own LMS (Amigo LMS Platform with Live Masterclasses & 1-on-1 Mentorship).
  • You want Data Analytics, Digital Marketing or IT as a specialization, which Symbiosis Online does not list.
  • Amity's brand recognition and large alumni base matter more to you than the fee difference.

Our take: Symbiosis lists a fee that is ₹39,000 (about 22%) lower and a higher NAAC grade. Amity's strengths are its mentorship format, brand and the Data Analytics, Digital Marketing and IT electives. Amity states a 40% minimum for graduation marks, while Symbiosis's listing states no minimum, so confirm the exact eligibility with both universities.
