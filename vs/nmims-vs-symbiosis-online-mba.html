Fees: NMIMS Online vs Symbiosis Online

NMIMS Online lists the Online MBA at ₹1,96,000 (₹49,000 per semester). Symbiosis Online lists ₹1,40,000 (₹35,000 per semester). Symbiosis Online is listed ₹56,000 (about 29%) cheaper in total.

On the 0% EMI plans as listed, the monthly amount is about ₹4,900 for NMIMS Online and ₹3,500 for Symbiosis Online (a difference of about ₹1,400 a month). Work out what suits your budget with our EMI calculator and check the payback with the ROI and salary calculator.

Which has the higher NAAC grade, NMIMS Online or Symbiosis Online?
