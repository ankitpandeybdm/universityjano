About SRM Institute of Science and Technology Online

With 28,000+ learners enrolled and a network of 400+ hiring partners, it is one of the more established names in Indian online higher education.

Frequently Asked Questions • SRM Online

Is a degree from SRM Online valid for Government Jobs & UPSC?expand_more

Yes, absolutely. Under University Grants Commission (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, degrees awarded through online mode by UGC-DEB recognized universities are treated as equivalent to traditional campus degrees for all Central and State Government jobs, UPSC, SSC CGL, PSUs, and defense recruitments.

Does the degree mention "Online" or "Distance Education"?expand_more

As per the official UGC mandate, the degree certificate awarded by SRM Institute of Science and Technology Online is identical in nomenclature and stature to on-campus degrees. It does not carry stigmatizing distance-mode labels, ensuring seamless corporate acceptance.

Do I ever need to visit Chennai or a physical exam center?expand_more

No. The entire lifecycle — including admissions, live classes, digital assignments, project submissions, and semester term-end examinations — is conducted 100% online. Exam mode: 100% Online Remote-Proctored Exams.
