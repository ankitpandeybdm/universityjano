Amity University Online FAQs

Is Amity University Online UGC-DEB approved?

Yes, Amity University Online is listed as UGC-DEB approved with NAAC A+ (3.27 CGPA). Always confirm the programme on the UGC-DEB portal for your admission session.

How many online programmes does Amity University Online offer?

The official fee sheet lists 38 programmes: 18 undergraduate, 16 postgraduate, 3 dual degree, 1 certificate. Fees range from ₹49,000 to ₹4,02,800.

What is the fee of the Online MBA at Amity University Online?

The official total fee for the general MBA is ₹2,25,000, paid as ₹56,300 in each of the first three semesters and ₹56,100 in the fourth. MBA tracks with specialisations are priced between ₹2,75,000 and ₹3,29,000. These are official fees without any discount.

What is the eligibility for the MBA at Amity University Online?

Graduation with 40% marks; candidates below 40% need to clear an entrance test. Please confirm the exact criteria with the university or a counsellor before applying.

Which is the cheapest and the costliest programme at Amity University Online?

The lowest fee is ₹49,000 (BFP (Certificate programme)) and the highest is ₹4,02,800 (BBA + MBA Dual Degree). Half of the programmes cost ₹2,50,000 or less.

How are exams conducted at Amity University Online?

100% Online Remote Proctored Exams.

Can I get a discount on the fees at Amity University Online?

The fees on this page are the official fees without any discount, scholarship or waiver. Connect with a counsellor to check which discounts or scholarships you may qualify for.

What is the highest package reported by Amity University Online?

The university reports a highest CTC of ₹18.5 LPA and an average CTC of ₹7.2 LPA. Placement support is not a job guarantee.
