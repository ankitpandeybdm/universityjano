NMIMS Centre for Distance & Online Education FAQs

Yes, NMIMS Centre for Distance & Online Education (CDOE) is listed as UGC-DEB approved with NAAC A+ (3.59 CGPA). Always confirm the programme on the UGC-DEB portal for your admission session.
