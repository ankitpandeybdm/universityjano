What is Online PGDM?

PG Diploma in Management (Online PGDM) is a UGC-DEB approved degree/diploma you can complete online, with recorded and live classes, online assignments and proctored or centre-based exams. It is designed for working professionals and students who need flexibility without relocating.

Online PGDM Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree from a UGC-recognised university
  • Minimum marks vary by university (commonly 45-50%, relaxation for reserved categories)
  • No entrance exam in most online programmes

Online PGDM FAQs

Is Online PGDM valid in India?

Yes, when the university and the programme are UGC-DEB approved for your admission session. Always verify on our UGC-DEB checker before paying any fee.

What is the duration of Online PGDM?

Usually 1-2 years, depending on the university and mode of study.

What is the fee of Online PGDM?

Roughly ₹60,000 – ₹2,00,000 for the full programme, depending on the university and specialisation. Check each university page for current fees.

PGDM vs MBA: what is the difference?

An MBA is awarded by a university; a PGDM is awarded by an autonomous institute. Check UGC-DEB / AICTE status of the specific online programme before applying.
