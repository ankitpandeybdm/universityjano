A counsellor decides whether an enquiry becomes an admission.

Students and parents have doubts about fees, approvals and value. The counsellor on the call is the one who answers them. When that person explains clearly, listens first and follows up on time, more enquiries turn into enrolments.

UniversityJano guides students through regular, online and certification courses every day. This training brings that counselling experience to your team.

What we train, in the order a conversation happens

Each stage of the counselling journey has its own skill. We work through them one by one with your team.

  1. Replying to the first enquiry

    Respond fast, sound helpful and start a real conversation instead of sending a brochure.

  2. Understanding the student

    Ask about goals, budget and situation before recommending any programme.

  3. Explaining the programme

    Present courses, eligibility, approvals and fees accurately, so students can trust what they hear.

  4. Handling objections

    Answer doubts about fees, approvals, value and timing with clear, honest replies.

  5. Talking about fees and EMI

    Explain the total fee and payment options plainly, so there are no surprises later.

  6. Following up

    Build a follow-up routine that keeps interested students moving towards admission.

  7. Closing the admission

    Guide the student through the final steps and make enrolment easy.

  8. Throughout: honest counselling. No false promises such as guaranteed placements, because trust protects your institution's name.
