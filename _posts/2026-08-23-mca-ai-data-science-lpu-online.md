<h2>Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>Requirements vary slightly by session, but generally you'll need:</p>
<ul>
<li>A bachelor's degree in any discipline, with a minimum of around 50% marks</li>
<li>Mathematics or Statistics studied at either the 10+2 level or during graduation</li>
<li>If you don't have a math/computer background, most sessions allow a bridge/foundation course to still qualify</li>
<li>No fixed age limit for admission</li>
</ul>
<p>BCA, B.Sc. (Computer Science/IT), and B.Com/B.A. graduates with a qualifying math background are all commonly eligible — the exact requirement can shift between admission cycles, so confirm your specific case before applying.</p>

<h2>Fees — What to Actually Expect</h2>

This is a genuinely wide range, so treat any number you see online — including this one — as indicative, not final.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Is LPU's Online MCA valid for government jobs and higher studies?</h3>
<p>Yes — LPU's Online MCA is UGC-entitled, which means it carries the same recognition as a regular MCA for employment and further academic pursuit in India.</p>

<h3>Do I need to appear for an entrance exam?</h3>
<p>For most online admission sessions, no — admission is merit-based on your prior academic record, though this can vary slightly by intake, so confirm for your specific session.</p>

<h3>Can I switch specializations after enrolling?</h3>
<p>Specializations are generally selected early in the program and are not something you casually switch mid-course — confirm the specific policy directly with LPU Online before applying if this matters to you.</p>

<h3>Is the fee the same for every specialization?</h3>
<p>No — fees can vary slightly by specialization and by which scholarships or grants apply to your specific admission session.</p>

<h2>Should You Choose This Program?</h2>
<p>An MCA in AI & Data Science makes the most sense if you already have some baseline comfort with programming and math, and you're specifically aiming for data-and-AI-adjacent roles rather than general software development. If you're earlier in your technical journey, it's worth comparing this against a more general MCA track, or even a data-science-focused MBA specialization, depending on whether you want to go deep technical or bridge into business-facing analytics roles.</p>
