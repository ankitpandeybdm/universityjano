<p>Sustainability, ESG and climate strategy have moved from the margins of business into boardrooms, and universities are starting to build management degrees around them. One of the newest is the <strong>MBA in Sustainability Management (Online)</strong> from TERI School of Advanced Studies (TERI SAS) in New Delhi, which opened admissions on 14 October 2026 for its first batch, with classes starting in <strong>January 2027</strong>. It is an unusual programme in two ways: it is positioned as an executive MBA for working professionals, and its published fee is far above the usual online MBA range. This guide summarises what the university has announced, what the numbers mean, who it suits, and what to verify before you apply.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Programme:</strong> MBA in Sustainability Management (Online), a 2-year programme with 88 credits across four semesters, offered through TERI SAS's Center for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) and Department of Management Studies.</li>
<li><strong>Who it is for:</strong> working professionals. According to the university's announcement, applicants need a graduation degree with at least 50% marks and at least <strong>three years of professional experience</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Published fee:</strong> ₹13,35,000 for all four semesters. The announcement mentions a limited 15% tuition waiver for eligible candidates, available to those who complete admission on or before 15 November 2026. That date has already passed, so ask whether any benefit remains.</li>
<li><strong>Approvals described:</strong> the announcement says the programme is UGC and AICTE approved. Verify this for your session on the official portals.</li>
<li><strong>Learning format:</strong> 100% online, with live and recorded sessions, workshops, industry interactions, and a 24-credit capstone project with industry immersion.</li>
<li><strong>Source note:</strong> the details above come from a university announcement published as sponsored content, so confirm everything on the official TERI SAS programme page before you decide.</li>
</ul>

<h2>About the Programme</h2>
<p>TERI SAS describes the programme as building on its established, classroom MBA in Sustainability Management and extending it online for professionals. It covers business strategy, Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG), climate change, sustainable finance and responsible business, with an interdisciplinary approach that connects management concepts to sustainability decisions in areas such as strategy, finance, operations, supply chains and risk management. TERI SAS is a UGC-recognised deemed-to-be university in New Delhi, established in 1998 by The Energy and Resources Institute and granted deemed status in 1999, according to the announcement. It also says applications from eligible international candidates are welcome. If you live abroad, read our guide on <a href="/blog/2026/11/18/online-mba-india-nri-indians-abroad-2027-eligibility-documents-fees/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">online MBA from India for NRIs and Indians abroad</a> for the questions to ask.</p>

<h2>The Fee in Context</h2>
<p>At ₹13.35 lakh, this programme costs several times what most standard online MBAs charge. For comparison, published ranges for standard UGC-DEB online MBAs cluster around ₹1 lakh to ₹3 lakh, as we explain in our guide to <a href="/blog/2026/11/17/online-mba-fees-india-2027-january-batch-total-cost/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">online MBA fees in India for 2027</a>, while online executive MBAs are listed in one comparison at ₹4 lakh to ₹15 lakh. So TERI SAS's fee sits at the upper end of the executive tier, which fits its positioning for experienced professionals and its practical components such as the capstone and industry immersion.</p>

<h2>Careers the Programme Points Toward</h2>
<p>The announcement says the knowledge may be relevant to roles such as sustainability manager, ESG analyst, sustainability consultant, sustainable finance specialist, CSR manager and climate strategy manager, depending on individual experience, skills and career goals. Treat this as a direction, not a promise. Hiring in this field depends on your background, the employer's priorities and the market, and many sustainability roles are filled by people who combine functional experience (finance, operations, supply chain, legal) with sustainability knowledge. That is exactly the profile a three-year experience requirement implies.</p>
<p>Be careful with salary and placement claims for any programme.
