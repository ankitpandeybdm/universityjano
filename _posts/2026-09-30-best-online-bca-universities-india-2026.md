<h2>A Simple Checklist for Judging Any Online BCA</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>UGC-DEB entitlement for your session.</strong> Check it with our <a href="{{ '/ugc-verifier.html' | relative_url }}" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">verifier</a>.</li>
<li><strong>A valid NAAC grade.</strong> Confirm it on the official NAAC site rather than the university's banner.</li>
<li><strong>All-inclusive fee.</strong> Ask for the total including registration, exam and project fees.</li>
<li><strong>Curriculum depth.</strong> Look for programming languages, databases, web development and a capstone project, not just an overview of subjects.</li>
<li><strong>Stable learning platform.</strong> Ask for a demo of the LMS and a sample live session.</li>
<li><strong>Real placement support.</strong> Ask for a placement report, not a partner-count figure.</li>
<li><strong>Clear admission timeline.</strong> Universities typically run January and July intakes, and you need a DEB ID before enrolling; see <a href="{{ '/blog/2026/09/30/deb-id-mandatory-online-degree-admission-2026/' | relative_url }}" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">how to create it</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Do I need mathematics in 12th for an online BCA?</h3>
<p>Requirements vary. Some universities ask for mathematics or business mathematics, while others admit any 10+2 stream. Check the eligibility for your session.</p>

<h3>Is an online BCA valid for higher studies?</h3>
<p>A BCA from a UGC-DEB entitled programme is treated as a regular degree, so it should qualify you for MCA and other postgraduate admissions, subject to each institution's criteria.</p>

<h3>Which online BCA is the cheapest?</h3>
<p>Central or state university options such as Mizoram University list the lowest fees, followed by options like GLA and Jamia Hamdard. Verify approval for your session first.</p>
