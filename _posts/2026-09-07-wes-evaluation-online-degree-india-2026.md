If you're planning to immigrate to Canada, apply for a master's program in the US, or take a job offer overseas, you've probably come across the term **WES evaluation** — and if you did your degree online in India, you're likely wondering whether it'll even qualify. This confusion is extremely common, and it usually comes from mixing up two completely separate approval systems: one that makes your degree valid *in India*, and one that makes it recognizable *abroad*.

This guide untangles both, so you know exactly what you need before you spend money on an evaluation.

## What Is WES Evaluation, Exactly?

WES (World Education Services) is a non-profit credential evaluation agency that converts your Indian degree into terms that US and Canadian institutions, employers, and immigration authorities can understand and trust. A WES evaluation report typically includes:

- **Degree equivalency** — a clear statement of what your Indian degree corresponds to in the US/Canadian academic system
- **Course-by-course credit evaluation** — a detailed breakdown of the credit value assigned to each subject on your transcript
- **Optional document authenticity verification** — for an additional fee, WES can verify your documents are genuine

A WES report is commonly required for **Canada's Express Entry immigration program**, admission to graduate programs in the US and Canada, and by international employers who need a standardized way to assess a foreign degree.

## The One Distinction That Clears Up Most of the Confusion

Here's the part almost every student gets wrong at first: **UGC-DEB approval and WES recognition solve two completely different problems.**

- **UGC-DEB approval** determines whether your online degree is legally valid **within India** — for government jobs, further studies, and standard employer verification here.
- **WES evaluation** only matters if you have specific plans involving **immigration, an overseas job, or further study abroad** in a country that uses the WES system (primarily the US and Canada). It has zero bearing on your degree's validity inside India.

In other words: a UGC-DEB approved online degree is already fully valid in India with or without WES. You only need to go through WES if and when you actually need your credentials evaluated for something happening outside India. Don't let anyone tell you a degree "isn't recognized" simply because it hasn't been WES-evaluated yet — that's a separate, optional step you take only when the situation calls for it.

## Does WES Accept Online / Distance Degrees from India?

Yes — WES does recognize online and distance education degrees from India, **provided the specific program is approved by UGC through its Distance Education Bureau (DEB)**, or by AICTE if it's a technical course. This is the exact same approval status our [free UGC-DEB Approval Checker](https://universityjano.com/ugc-verifier.html) confirms for you — so if you've already verified your program that way for your own peace of mind in India, you've done most of the groundwork WES will also expect to see reflected in your documents.

**What WES will not accept:** degrees from institutions that aren't recognized by UGC, AICTE, or an equivalent statutory authority — commonly referred to as degree mills. There's no publicly published "pre-approved WES list" of Indian universities; WES evaluates each application's supporting documents individually, which is exactly why starting with a genuinely UGC-DEB approved program matters so much before you invest years and money into a degree.

## Step-by-Step: How the WES Evaluation Process Works

1. **Confirm your university and program are UGC-DEB approved** (or AICTE-approved for technical courses) before you apply for evaluation — this is the single biggest factor in a smooth approval.
2. **Create your WES account** and select the correct evaluation type for your purpose (study, employment, or Canada immigration/Express Entry — each has a slightly different report format).
3. **Request your university send sealed transcripts directly to WES.** Self-attested or personally submitted copies are generally not accepted — the transcript must come directly from the issuing university in a sealed envelope, with your WES reference number written on it.
4. **Submit your final degree certificate** along with your transcripts.
5. **Pay the evaluation fee** — accepted via credit card, debit card, or PayPal on the official WES portal. Fees vary depending on the report type you need.
6. **Track your application** through your WES dashboard, where you can monitor document acceptance and processing status.
7. **Receive your digital report**, which you can then send electronically to universities, employers, or immigration authorities like IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada).

The entire process typically takes **1-2 months** from document submission to final report, so if you're working against an immigration or admission deadline, start well in advance rather than waiting until the last minute.

## How Long Is a WES Report Valid?

For **study and employment purposes**, WES reports generally don't expire. For **Canada immigration (Express Entry)** specifically, your Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) is valid for **5 years** from the date of issue — if your immigration timeline stretches beyond that, you'll need a fresh evaluation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need WES evaluation to get a job in India with my online degree?**
No. WES evaluation is only relevant for opportunities outside India — immigration, overseas study, or international employment. Within India, your degree's validity depends entirely on UGC-DEB approval.

**Can I get a WES evaluation for any online degree from India?**
Only if the university and your specific program hold valid UGC-DEB approval (or AICTE approval for technical courses). Degrees from unrecognized institutions will not be accepted by WES.

**How much does a WES evaluation cost?**
Fees vary depending on the type of report you need (course-by-course, document-by-document, or ECA for Canada immigration) and are payable via credit card, debit card, or PayPal directly on the official WES website.

**Is a WES evaluation the same as getting my degree "recognized" in India?**
No — these are unrelated. Your degree's recognition in India comes from UGC-DEB approval alone. WES evaluation is a separate step that only matters for plans involving another country.
