<p>Amrita AHEAD is the online learning division of <strong>Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham</strong>, a deemed-to-be university founded in 2003 with campuses across Coimbatore, Kochi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Amritapuri and Mysuru. The parent university holds some of the strongest institutional credentials of any online provider in India:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NIRF Rank #7 overall in India</strong> — genuinely elite territory, not a marketing exaggeration</li>
<li><strong>NAAC A++ (3.70 CGPA)</strong> — the top legacy accreditation grade</li>
<li><strong>UGC-DEB approved</strong> for the 2026-27 session, plus AICTE approval</li>
<li><strong>WES recognized</strong> and an AIU member — relevant if study or immigration abroad is on your radar</li>
</ul>
<p>Classes run on the Amrita AHEAD LMS with live lectures from Amrita's own faculty (not a separate contracted teaching pool, which is common at some other online providers), and the MBA program specifically incorporates a Harvard case-study teaching methodology.</p>

<h2>Undergraduate Programs: BBA, BCA, BCom</h2>
<p><strong>BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration):</strong> a 3-year foundation in management — marketing, finance, HR and operations basics — for students coming straight from 12th grade or graduates from an unrelated field who want a management foundation. Eligibility is a pass in 10+2 from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate typically around 50%.</p>
<p><strong>BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications):</strong> a 3-year computer science foundation covering programming, database management and networking, aimed at students heading into software development, web development or IT support roles. Eligibility is a pass in 10+2 with a minimum of around 50% aggregate — no prior coding background required, since the program starts from fundamentals.</p>
<p><strong>BCom (Bachelor of Commerce):</strong> a 3-year commerce and accounting foundation, the standard entry point for students aiming at finance, accounting or a later MCom/MBA. Eligibility follows the same 10+2 pattern as BBA and BCA.</p>

<h2>Postgraduate Programs: MBA, MCA, MCom</h2>
<p><strong>MBA:</strong> Amrita's flagship online program, offered with specializations in General Management, Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations, and AI for Business, plus a Harvard case-study teaching approach layered across the core curriculum. Reported outcomes: 94% placement rate, ₹20 LPA highest package, ₹8.8 LPA average package, backed by 450+ hiring partners including Amazon, Microsoft, Cisco, Accenture, TCS, Cognizant, Deloitte and Oracle. Eligibility is a bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of around 50% aggregate — no entrance exam required for the online mode.</p>
<p><strong>MCA:</strong> offered as a general track or with an Artificial Intelligence &amp; Machine Learning specialization, aimed at students who want to build technical depth rather than management breadth. Eligibility typically requires a bachelor's degree with Mathematics or a computing background, either at 10+2 or graduation level — confirm the exact requirement for your session, since it's stricter than the MBA's open-discipline policy.</p>
<p><strong>MCom:</strong> offered in two specializations — <strong>Finance &amp; Systems</strong> and <strong>International Finance &amp; Accounting</strong>, with the latter notably built to meet CPA (USA) curriculum standards, a genuinely useful detail if you're considering an international accounting career. Eligibility is a bachelor's degree with a minimum of around 50% aggregate.</p>

<h2>Scholarships and Fee Support</h2>
<p>Amrita offers a <strong>Chancellor's Merit Scholarship of up to 25%</strong> for eligible students based on academic merit, applicable across its online programs. <h3>Is Amrita University Online valid for government jobs and higher studies?</h3>
<p>Yes — Amrita AHEAD is UGC-DEB approved (for the 2026-27 session) and AICTE approved, which means its online degrees carry the same recognition as regular degrees for employment and further academic pursuit in India.
