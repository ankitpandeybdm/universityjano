<h2>What "Zero-Cost EMI" Usually Means</h2>
<p>In most cases, zero-cost EMI means you are not charged separate interest on the instalments. The financing is typically provided through a partner lender, and the cost of that financing is absorbed somewhere else: built into the fee, taken from a discount you no longer receive, or offset by other charges. Because the arrangement differs by university and lender, do not assume it is identical everywhere.</p>

<h2>Five Questions to Ask Before You Take an EMI</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>What is the total amount I will pay?</strong> Multiply the monthly EMI by the number of months and compare it with the listed fee. Any difference is a hidden cost. Our <a href="{{ '/emi-calculator.html' | relative_url }}" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">EMI calculator</a> can help you test the numbers.</li>
<li><strong>Is there a processing fee, GST or foreclosure charge?</strong> Zero interest does not always mean zero charges.</li>
<li><strong>Is there a discount for paying upfront?</strong> If a university offers, say, 8–12% off for full payment, the EMI route effectively costs more.</li>
<li><strong>Who is the lender, and what happens if I miss an instalment?</strong> The EMI agreement is often with a third-party lender, not the university.</li>
<li><strong>What happens to the EMI if I withdraw?</strong> Ask for the refund policy in writing.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Is 0% EMI really free?</h3>
<p>Not always. The financing cost may be included in the fee, offset by a discount you forgo, or partly recovered through processing charges. Ask for the total payable amount in writing.</p>

<h3>Which online university offers the lowest monthly EMI?</h3>
<p>The monthly figure depends on the total fee and tenure, so cheaper programmes naturally have lower EMIs. GLA advertises one of the lowest starting instalments among private universities. Compare total cost, not just the monthly amount.</p>

<h3>Do I need a good credit score for zero-cost EMI?</h3>
<p>Partner lenders may apply their own eligibility checks, so approval is not guaranteed for every applicant. Confirm the lender's requirements before you rely on EMI to fund your admission.</p>
