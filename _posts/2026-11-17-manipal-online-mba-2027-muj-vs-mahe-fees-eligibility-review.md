<p>"Manipal Online MBA" is one of the most searched names in online management education, but there is a catch that trips up many applicants: <strong>there is more than one Manipal university offering an online MBA</strong>. Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) is the one most people mean, but the Online Manipal website also lists an online MBA from Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE). On top of that, published fee figures for the MUJ programme range from about ₹1.66 lakh to ₹1.8 lakh. This guide untangles the names, summarises what third-party and official pages say about fees, eligibility and specialisations for the <strong>January 2027 batch</strong>, and gives you a checklist to confirm everything before you pay.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Programme (MUJ):</strong> a 2-year (24-month, 4-semester) online MBA, with up to 4 years allowed to complete according to Online Manipal.</li>
<li><strong>Which Manipal?</strong> Online Manipal pages show an MBA from <strong>Manipal University Jaipur</strong> and a separate MBA page for <strong>MAHE</strong>. Always check the name of the university on your admission letter, fee receipt and degree certificate.</li>
<li><strong>Published fee (MUJ):</strong> about ₹1,66,000 on one portal listing, about ₹1,75,000 on others, and about ₹1,80,000 on several. Online Manipal's own page shows ₹43,750 per semester, which is ₹1,75,000 over four semesters, while another page shows ₹45,000 per semester. An application fee of ₹500 is listed on one page.</li>
<li><strong>Approvals described:</strong> UGC-entitled online degree, NAAC A+ for MUJ, and an NBA-accredited MBA programme on the official programme page. Verify each on the official portals for your session.</li>
<li><strong>Eligibility (official page):</strong> a minimum of 50% marks in graduation.</li>
<li><strong>Specialisations:</strong> pages say 10 or 13, with an optional dual-specialisation route, so confirm the current list.</li>
</ul>

<p>The rule is simple: approvals, fees, accreditation and your degree certificate belong to the specific university that awards the degree. A grade or ranking quoted for one does not carry over to the other. If a counsellor says "Manipal", ask for the full university name in writing. This blog focuses on the <strong>MUJ</strong> online MBA, which is what most pages describe.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is the fee for Manipal Online MBA?</h3>
<p>Pages quote about ₹1.66 lakh to ₹1.8 lakh for the MUJ programme in total. Ask MUJ for the written schedule for the January 2027 batch.</p>

<h3>Is Manipal University Jaipur Online MBA UGC approved?</h3>
<p>MUJ's online MBA is described as UGC-entitled. Verify the programme and session on the official UGC-DEB portal before paying.</p>

<h3>What is the difference between MUJ and MAHE online MBA?</h3>
<p>They are different universities in different states, with separate approvals, fees and degree certificates. Always confirm which one is awarding your degree.</p>

<h3>What is the eligibility for MUJ Online MBA?</h3>
<p>Online Manipal states a minimum of 50% marks in graduation. Confirm any relaxation for your category.</p>

<h3>Is MUJ NAAC A+ accredited?</h3>
<p>Pages consistently describe MUJ as NAAC A+. Confirm the grade and validity on the NAAC website.</p>

<h3>How many specialisations does the MUJ Online MBA have?</h3>
<p>Pages say 10 or 13, so confirm the current list for the January 2027 session.</p>

<h3>Does Manipal Online MBA offer dual specialisation?</h3>
<p>A portal summary says yes. Ask which combinations are allowed and whether both appear on the degree.</p>

<h3>Is Manipal Online MBA worth it?</h3>
<p>It can be a sensible choice if the programme is approved for your session, the fee fits your budget and the specialisation matches your goal. Compare it with at least two other universities.</p>
