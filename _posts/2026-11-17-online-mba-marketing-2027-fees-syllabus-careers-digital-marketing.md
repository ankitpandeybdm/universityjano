<p>Marketing is one of the most popular online MBA specialisations, partly because it is open to people from every background and partly because marketing work is visible: you can show campaigns, results and a portfolio. But the field has also changed fast. Brand, sales and digital marketing now overlap, tools matter as much as theory, and employers care more about what you have done than the degree name. This guide covers the <strong>online MBA in Marketing</strong> for the <strong>January 2027 batch</strong>: published fee ranges, eligibility, syllabus, digital marketing options, careers, and the practical skills and portfolio you should build alongside your classes.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Duration:</strong> 2 years (4 semesters).</li>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> a bachelor's degree from a recognised university, usually with about 50% marks. Any stream is accepted at most universities.</li>
<li><strong>Published fee ranges (third-party estimates):</strong> one education portal's specialisation page puts online MBA in Marketing at roughly ₹50,000 to ₹3,70,000 across universities, and online MBA in Digital Marketing at roughly ₹73,500 to ₹2,25,000.</li>
<li><strong>Same price as other tracks?</strong> At many universities marketing costs the same as finance and HR. One private university's schedule shows ₹2,00,000 for standard specialisations, with premium tracks priced higher.</li>
<li><strong>Valid?</strong> Yes, when the university and programme are UGC-DEB approved for your session, with AICTE approval where applicable.</li>
<li><strong>What decides outcomes:</strong> a portfolio of real campaigns or projects, working knowledge of marketing tools, and relevant experience, more than the degree alone.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What to Build Beyond the Degree</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>A portfolio of 3 to 5 projects:</strong> a campaign plan, a content calendar with results, a brand audit, or a small paid-ads test with real numbers.</li>
<li><strong>Tool skills:</strong> web analytics, an ads platform, an email or CRM tool and basic spreadsheet analysis.</li>
<li><strong>Writing and presentation:</strong> marketing roles reward clear communication.</li>
<li><strong>Hands-on exposure:</strong> an internship, freelance work or a role in your current company that touches marketing.</li>
<li><strong>A visible profile:</strong> a LinkedIn presence or a portfolio page that shows your work.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is the fee for an online MBA in marketing?</h3>
<p>One portal's listing puts it at roughly ₹50,000 to ₹3,70,000 across universities. Get the total for your batch in writing.</p>

<h3>What is the fee for an online MBA in digital marketing?</h3>
<p>The same source puts it at roughly ₹73,500 to ₹2,25,000, depending on the college.</p>

<h3>What is the eligibility for online MBA in marketing?</h3>
<p>A bachelor's degree from a recognised university, commonly with about 50% marks. Confirm your university's rule.</p>

<h3>Is online MBA in marketing valid?</h3>
<p>Yes, when the university and programme are UGC-DEB approved for your session, with AICTE approval where applicable.</p>

<h3>Is MBA in marketing better than digital marketing?</h3>
<p>Neither is better in general. A general marketing MBA covers brand, sales and strategy, while a digital track focuses on online channels. Choose by your target role.</p>

<h3>Can a fresher get a marketing job after an online MBA?</h3>
<p>The degree helps, but employers also look for internships, projects and tool skills. Build a portfolio while you study.</p>

<h3>Do I need prior marketing experience?</h3>
<p>No. Most programmes accept graduates from any stream. Experience helps you in class and in hiring.</p>

<h3>Is the fee for marketing higher than other specialisations?</h3>
<p>At many universities it is the same, but premium tracks can cost more. Ask for your exact track's fee.</p>

<h3>When do admissions open for the January 2027 batch?</h3>
<p>Many universities are taking applications now. Check each university's closing date and apply early.</p>
