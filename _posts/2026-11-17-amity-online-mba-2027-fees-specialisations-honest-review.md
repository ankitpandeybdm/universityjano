<p>Amity is among the first names that come up when people shortlist an online MBA, and it advertises a long specialisation list, zero-cost EMI and international accreditations. But try to pin down the fee for the <strong>January 2027 batch</strong> and you will find something unusual: even pages published by Amity and its partners show different totals, such as ₹1.99 lakh, ₹2.07 lakh and ₹2.25 lakh, and a dual specialisation that can cost close to ₹3 lakh or more. This guide explains why, covers eligibility, specialisations and accreditation claims, and gives you a checklist for paying safely.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Programme:</strong> a 2-year (4-semester) fully online MBA with live and recorded classes.</li>
<li><strong>Published fee for a single specialisation:</strong> ₹2,25,000 in total (₹56,300 per semester) on several portals, ₹2,07,000 on one Amity page for a one-time payment, and ₹1,99,000 (₹49,750 per semester) on an Amity blog and a brochure-based page.</li>
<li><strong>Dual specialisation and certification-linked tracks:</strong> pages quote ₹2,99,000 and ₹3,29,000, and one portal says the range is ₹2,25,000 to ₹4,49,000 depending on specialisation.</li>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> an Amity blog states graduation in any discipline with at least 40% marks, while other pages say graduation from a recognised university with no fixed percentage. Confirm for your batch.</li>
<li><strong>Specialisations:</strong> pages say 10+, 12 and 18+, so confirm the current list.</li>
<li><strong>Approvals described:</strong> UGC-DEB entitled, AICTE recognised and NAAC A+. International claims (WASC, QAA, WES, QS) appear too and should be treated as claims to test.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Eligibility and Admission</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Qualification:</strong> a bachelor's degree. An Amity blog states at least 40% marks, while other pages do not give a figure. Confirm your category's rule.</li>
<li><strong>Entrance exam:</strong> published guides do not mention one. Confirm for your batch.</li>
<li><strong>Work experience:</strong> generally not required for the standard programme. See our <a href="/blog/2026/11/10/online-mba-without-work-experience-freshers-2026/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">online MBA for freshers guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>IDs:</strong> a valid ABC ID and DEB-ID are needed before enrolment. See the <a href="/blog/2026/09/30/deb-id-mandatory-online-degree-admission-2026/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">DEB ID guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Dates:</strong> pages for the earlier session gave application windows in mid-2026. We have not found a confirmed UGC last date for the January 2027 session, and Amity sets its own closing date. <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is the fee for Amity Online MBA?</h3>
<p>Pages show ₹2,25,000 as the standard total, ₹2,07,000 for a one-time payment on one page, and ₹1,99,000 on an Amity blog. Dual and certification-linked tracks are higher. Get the written fee for your batch.</p>

<h3>Is Amity Online MBA UGC approved?</h3>
<p>Pages describe it as UGC-DEB entitled. Verify the programme for your session on the official portal before paying.</p>

<h3>What is the eligibility for Amity Online MBA?</h3>
<p>An Amity blog states graduation with at least 40% marks, and other pages say graduation from a recognised university. Confirm for your batch and category.</p>

<h3>How much is Amity Online dual specialisation MBA?</h3>
<p>Pages quote ₹2,99,000 and ₹3,29,000. Ask for the exact fee and the allowed combinations.</p>

<h3>How many specialisations does Amity Online MBA have?</h3>
<p>Pages say 10+, 12 or 18+. Confirm the current list for the January 2027 session.</p>

<h3>Is Amity Online MBA recognised abroad?</h3>
<p>Some pages mention WES, WASC and QAA. WES evaluates credentials case by case, so treat this as a claim to test. See our WES guide.</p>

<h3>Is zero-cost EMI really zero cost at Amity?</h3>
<p>Check whether the EMI price equals the one-time price and whether processing fees apply. Compare the total you will pay under each option.</p>

<h3>Is Amity Online MBA worth it?</h3>
