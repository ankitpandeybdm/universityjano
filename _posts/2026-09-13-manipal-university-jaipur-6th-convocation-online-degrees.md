<p>On September 12, 2026, Manipal University Jaipur's (MUJ) Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) held its <strong>6th Convocation Ceremony</strong>, awarding degrees to <strong>5,655 online learners</strong> across MBA, BBA, MCA, BCA, MA (JMC), M.Com. and B.Com. programmes. If you're currently studying with Online Manipal, or weighing whether to enrol, here's what actually happened at the ceremony — and why an event like this is worth paying attention to before you pick an online university.</p>

<h2>What Happened at the 6th Convocation</h2>
<p>Of the 5,655 graduates conferred degrees, 903 received them in person on stage from the ceremony's dignitaries, while the remaining degrees were conferred in absentia. The graduating cohort included learners based outside India, and reportedly spanned an age range from 19 to 78 — a reminder that online degree programmes at this scale serve everyone from fresh graduates to career-long professionals returning to education.</p>
<p>The ceremony was presided over by Dr. N. N. Sharma, President of Manipal University Jaipur, alongside Cdr. (Dr.) Anil Rana (Pro-President), Dr. Amit Soni (Registrar), Dr. Nitu Bhatnagar (Provost), Dr. Mallikarjuna Gadapa (Director, CDOE) and Mr. Dasari Nagaraju (Controller of Examinations). The Chief Guest was Mr. Ashish Kumar Srivastava, Managing Director of Haver &amp; Boecker India, with Mr. Ambrish Sinha, CEO of UNext Learning, attending as Guest of Honour. The proceedings opened with the University Kulgeet before the formal conferment of degrees began.</p>

<h2>Why a Convocation Like This Actually Matters to Prospective Students</h2>
<p>It's easy to treat a convocation as a ceremonial photo-op, but for anyone evaluating an online degree, an event like this is genuinely useful evidence — not marketing copy. A few things worth noting:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Scale signals a mature, running program.</strong> A single batch of 5,655 graduates across seven programmes shows this isn't a small pilot — it's an established, high-volume online education operation with years of enrollment and delivery behind it.</li>
<li><strong>In-person conferment matters.</strong> Roughly one in six graduates travelled to receive their degree on stage, which suggests the ceremony — and the credential — is treated as a real milestone, not a formality mailed out later.</li>
<li><strong>A wide age range reflects real-world flexibility.</strong> Learners from 19 to 78 in the same cohort is a practical demonstration that the online format genuinely accommodates both fresh graduates and working or retired professionals, not just one segment.</li>
<li><strong>Industry presence at the podium is a signal worth reading.</strong> A Chief Guest from manufacturing (Haver &amp; Boecker India) and a Guest of Honour from ed-tech (UNext Learning) suggests the university is actively building bridges between its graduates and employers, not operating in isolation.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Where This Fits If You're Comparing Online Manipal to Other Universities</h2>
<p>A convocation is a good trust signal, but it's still only one input. Before shortlisting any online MBA, MCA, BBA or BCA programme — including Online Manipal — it's worth checking the same three things regardless of how impressive the ceremony looked:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Current-session UGC-DEB approval</strong> for the specific program and year you'd be enrolling in, since approval is granted per session and can change.</li>
<li><strong>Real fees, NAAC grade, and placement data</strong> compared side by side against other approved universities, not read off a single brochure.</li>
<li><strong>What the degree actually costs per month</strong> once EMI options are factored in, not just the headline total fee.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Is an online degree from Manipal University Jaipur valid for jobs and further studies?</h3>
<p>Yes — MUJ's online programmes are offered under UGC-DEB regulations, which gives them the same legal standing as on-campus degrees for employment and higher education, provided the specific program and session were approved at the time of enrollment.</p>

<h3>Does MUJ conduct convocation ceremonies every year?</h3>
<p>Yes. This was MUJ's 6th Convocation for its Centre for Distance and Online Education, indicating the university holds this ceremony on a regular annual or cyclical basis as batches complete their programmes.</p>

<h3>Can I attend the convocation in person even if I studied entirely online?</h3>
<p>Based on this ceremony, yes — 903 of the 5,655 graduates received their degrees in person, confirming that online learners are invited and able to attend the physical ceremony if they choose to.</p>

<h3>How do I know if next year's admission cycle at MUJ is still UGC-DEB approved?</h3>
<p>Approval is granted per program and academic session, so it's worth confirming before every enrollment cycle using a <a href="/ugc-verifier.html" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">free UGC-DEB Approval Checker</a> rather than assuming last year's approval carries forward automatically.</p>
