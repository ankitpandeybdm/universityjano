<p>Every company that makes, stocks, sells or moves goods depends on operations and supply chain management, and those roles have become more visible as e-commerce, manufacturing and logistics have grown. An <strong>online MBA in Operations and Supply Chain Management</strong> is a practical choice for people already working in or around these functions, and for engineers and commerce graduates who want to move into planning and management roles. This guide covers the <strong>January 2027 batch</strong>: published fee ranges, eligibility, syllabus, careers, the tools worth learning, and how to choose a programme without paying for features you will not use.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Duration:</strong> 2 years (4 semesters).</li>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> a bachelor's degree from a recognised university, usually with about 50% marks. Published guides note there is generally no age limit, and that most online MBAs admit without an entrance exam.</li>
<li><strong>Published fee ranges (third-party estimates):</strong> one education portal puts online MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management at roughly ₹50,000 to ₹2,16,000 across more than 15 UGC-approved colleges, and Operations Management at an average of roughly ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,20,000. Another guide lists ₹13,000 to ₹50,000 per semester depending on the university.</li>
<li><strong>Examples published online:</strong> one central university's online MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management is listed at ₹56,690 in total on a partner site, and one private university lists Operations and Supply Chain Management among its online MBA specialisations at ₹1,60,000 for 2026-27 in a sponsored article. Verify these on the universities' own pages.</li>
<li><strong>Valid?</strong> Yes, when the university and programme are UGC-DEB approved for your session, with AICTE approval where applicable.</li>
<li><strong>What decides outcomes:</strong> your hands-on experience, tool skills and the problems you can show you have solved, more than the degree alone.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Who Should Choose It?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Operations, logistics, warehousing and procurement professionals</strong> who want a management credential for promotion.</li>
<li><strong>Manufacturing and quality professionals</strong> moving from the shop floor or plant into planning and management.</li>
<li><strong>Engineers</strong> (especially mechanical, industrial and production) who want business skills. See our <a href="/blog/2026/11/17/online-mba-for-engineers-btech-2027-eligibility-specialisation-guide/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">online MBA for engineers guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>E-commerce, retail and FMCG professionals</strong> working with inventory, fulfilment and distribution.</li>
<li><strong>Entrepreneurs and family-business owners</strong> who run production or trading businesses.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Eligibility and Admission</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Qualification:</strong> a bachelor's degree in any stream at most universities. Engineers, commerce and science graduates are all common applicants.</li>
<li><strong>Marks:</strong> commonly about 50% in graduation. Confirm any relaxation for your category.</li>
<li><strong>Entrance exam:</strong> most online MBAs admit on eligibility and verification, with CAT-type exams optional or unnecessary at many universities.</li>
<li><strong>Experience:</strong> not required for standard online MBAs, though it helps a lot in this specialisation. See our <a href="/blog/2026/11/10/online-mba-without-work-experience-freshers-2026/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">freshers guide</a>.</li>

<h2>Skills and Tools to Build Alongside the Degree</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Advanced Excel</strong> for forecasting, inventory and cost analysis. It is the entry ticket to most planning roles.</li>
<li><strong>An ERP system</strong> such as SAP or a similar platform, even at a basic level. Many programmes include ERP, but practical exposure at work is stronger.</li>
<li><strong>Data analysis:</strong> SQL and a BI tool help you turn operational data into decisions. See our <a href="/blog/2026/11/17/online-mba-business-analytics-2027-fees-syllabus-career-scope/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">business analytics guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Process improvement methods</strong> such as Lean or Six Sigma, if relevant to your industry.</li>
<li><strong>A portfolio of results:</strong> a cost saving, a lead-time reduction or a process you improved, with numbers.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is the fee for an online MBA in operations and supply chain management?</h3>
<p>One portal lists logistics and supply chain at roughly ₹50,000 to ₹2,16,000 and operations management at an average of ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,20,000. Get the written total for your batch.</p>

<h3>What is the eligibility for online MBA in operations?</h3>
<p>A bachelor's degree from a recognised university, commonly with about 50% marks, in any stream at most universities. Confirm your university's rule.</p>

<h3>Is an online MBA in supply chain valid?</h3>
<p>Yes, when the university and programme are UGC-DEB approved for your session, with AICTE approval where applicable.</p>

<h3>Which subjects are in online MBA operations?</h3>
<p>Core management subjects plus operations management, logistics and supply chain, operations research, quality, ERP and project management, depending on the university.</p>

<h3>Which jobs can I get after it?</h3>
<p>Roles such as operations manager, supply chain manager, logistics manager, procurement specialist and quality manager, depending on your experience and skills.</p>

<h3>Is it good for engineers?</h3>
<p>Yes, especially mechanical, industrial and production engineers moving into management. See our online MBA for engineers guide.</p>

<h3>Do I need work experience?</h3>
<p>Not for standard online MBAs, but experience in operations or logistics makes the specialisation far more valuable.</p>

<h3>Is the fee different for operations than for other specialisations?</h3>
<p>At many universities it is the same. Ask for the fee for your exact track.</p>

<h3>When do admissions open for the January 2027 batch?</h3>
<p>Many universities are taking applications now. Check each university's closing date and apply early.</p>
