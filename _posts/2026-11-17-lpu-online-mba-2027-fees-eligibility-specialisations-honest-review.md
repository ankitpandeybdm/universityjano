<p>Lovely Professional University (LPU) is one of the names students shortlist most often when they search for an online MBA in India. It appears in almost every comparison, and for good reason: it has a large online arm, a long specialisation list and a published fee that looks competitive. But if you try to find out what the LPU Online MBA costs for the <strong>January 2027 batch</strong>, you will quickly run into a problem: third-party pages publish very different totals for the same programme. This guide shows that spread, explains what usually causes it, covers eligibility, specialisations, pros and cons, and tells you exactly what to verify before you pay.</p>

<h2>Quick Answer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Programme:</strong> a 2-year (4-semester) online MBA, with live and recorded classes, and batches that start every six months (January and July) according to published guides.</li>
<li><strong>Approvals described by LPU and third-party pages:</strong> UGC-DEB entitlement for online programmes, AICTE approval for the MBA, and NAAC A++ accreditation. Please confirm each one on the official UGC-DEB, AICTE and NAAC portals for your session, as we explain below.</li>
<li><strong>Published total fee:</strong> the numbers range from about ₹1.3 lakh to about ₹2 lakh across pages. Your price is the written schedule for your specialisation and batch.</li>
<li><strong>Specialisations:</strong> pages disagree on the count (9 on some pages, 12 on others), which is another reason to confirm the current list.</li>
<li><strong>Exam fee:</strong> pages mention ₹2,000 per semester. Whether it sits inside the semester fee or on top is described differently, so ask.</li>
<li><strong>Verdict in one line:</strong> a widely shortlisted, well-known option that is worth considering, provided you verify approval for your session and compare the real total with other universities.</li>
</ul>

<p>That is a gap of nearly ₹70,000 between the lowest and highest published totals, for what is advertised as one programme. The usual reasons:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Different specialisations or batches.</strong> One page notes different semester fees for different programmes within the same university.</li>
<li><strong>Time-limited grants and discounts.</strong> A 20% grant described for an earlier deadline in 2026 cannot be assumed to apply to the January 2027 batch.</li>
<li><strong>Lump sum versus instalments.</strong> Paying everything upfront can be cheaper than semester-wise or EMI payment.</li>
<li><strong>Exam and registration fees.</strong> Pages describe the exam fee as included in some cases and separate in others, and the one-time registration fee as ₹600 on one page and ₹1,000 on another.</li>
<li><strong>Old pages.</strong> Many articles are not updated each session.</li>
</ul>
<p>The takeaway: <strong>do not decide on a fee you read on a blog.</strong> Ask LPU for the written fee schedule for your specialisation and the January 2027 batch, and compare it with our guide to <a href="/blog/2026/11/17/online-mba-fees-india-2027-january-batch-total-cost/" style="color:#1d4ed8;font-weight:600;">online MBA fees in India for 2027</a>.</p>
