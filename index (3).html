Frequently Asked Questions

What is UGC-DEB approval and why does it matter for an online degree?expand_more
UGC-DEB approval is what makes an online degree legally equivalent to a regular on-campus degree for jobs, higher education, and government exams. Always verify current-session approval for your exact program before paying any fee.
How much does an online MBA cost in India in 2026-27?expand_more
Typically between ₹50,000 and ₹2,50,000 for the full two-year program, depending on the university and specialization, with 0% EMI options at most approved universities. Compare real fees here.
Will 'Online Mode' be written on my official degree certificate?expand_more
As per UGC regulations, the certificate mentions the program name without stigmatizing differences, under the UGC (ODL & Online Programmes) Regulations 2020, which establishes equal statutory parity with on-campus awards.
Is UniversityJano's counselling and comparison service really free?expand_more
Yes — comparing universities, checking UGC-DEB approval, running fee and ROI calculators, and speaking with counsellors on UniversityJano.com is completely free for students.
Do employers in India accept online degrees?expand_more
Yes, most Indian employers now accept UGC-DEB approved online degrees at par with regular degrees, since they carry the same legal recognition.
