Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ 'now' | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}

UniversityJano.com ("we", "us", "our") is an independent educational technology discovery and comparison platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you use our university comparison tools, calculators, the AI College Finder wizard, or request free counselling, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, city, and the courses or universities you're interested in.

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect you with free 1-on-1 counselling about universities and courses you've shown interest in.
  • To share relevant fee, admission, EMI and placement information for the universities you compare.
  • To improve our tools, comparison data, and overall website experience.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, email or SMS regarding your enquiry.

Information Sharing

We may share your enquiry details with the specific UGC-DEB approved universities or their authorized admission partners you express interest in, solely so they can assist you with admissions. We do not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties.

Your Choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by emailing help@universityjano.com or calling our helpline at 1800-890-5266.

Cookies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use our tools, so we can improve them over time.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, write to us at help@universityjano.com.
