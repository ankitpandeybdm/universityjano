<h2>Why Amrita AHEAD for an Online MBA?</h2>
<p>Amrita is ranked <strong>#7 overall in India by NIRF</strong> and holds <strong>NAAC A++ accreditation</strong> (3.70 CGPA, Category 1 Autonomy status) — both are among the strongest credentials of any university offering an online MBA in India. It's also UGC-DEB approved, AICTE approved, WES recognized, and an AIU member. Classes run through the Amrita AHEAD LMS with live lectures from Amrita's own faculty, and the program includes a Harvard case-study teaching methodology alongside its core coursework.</p>

<h2>Eligibility Criteria</h2>
<p>Requirements can shift slightly by admission cycle, but generally you'll need:</p>
<ul>
<li>A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university, with a minimum of around 50% aggregate marks</li>
<li>No entrance exam required for the online program</li>
<li>Working professionals are fully eligible and form a significant part of the cohort</li>
<li>No fixed age limit for admission</li>
</ul>
<p>Given Amrita's strong NIRF ranking and NAAC A++ grade, expect the overall academic rigor and evaluation standards to be on the stricter side compared to some other online MBA providers — this is consistent with the university's broader academic reputation and is worth factoring in alongside the eligibility numbers.</p>

<h2>Fees — What to Actually Expect</h2>
<p>Amrita AHEAD's Online MBA carries a total program fee of approximately <strong>₹1,70,000</strong> for the full two-year program, working out to around ₹42,500 per semester across 4 semesters. No-cost EMI is available, starting from roughly ₹4,250/month. Amrita also offers a Chancellor's Merit Scholarship of up to 25% for eligible students based on academic merit. Treat this as an indicative figure — fee structures can shift by admission session, so confirm the exact current number before you apply.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>Is Amrita AHEAD's Online MBA valid for government jobs and higher studies?</h3>
<p>Yes — the program is UGC-DEB approved (for the 2026-27 session) and AICTE approved, which means it carries the same recognition as a regular MBA for employment and further academic pursuit in India.</p>

<h3>Do I need to appear for an entrance exam?</h3>
<p>No — admission to Amrita AHEAD's Online MBA is merit-based on your prior academic record, with no CAT, MAT or similar exam required.</p>

<h3>Is Amrita's online MBA harder than other online MBA programs?</h3>
<p>Given Amrita's NIRF #7 ranking and NAAC A++ accreditation, evaluation standards tend to be on the stricter side compared to some other online MBA providers — this reflects the university's broader academic reputation for rigor.</p>

<h3>Is Amrita's online degree recognized outside India?</h3>
<p>Amrita holds WES recognition, which is relevant if you're considering study or immigration to Canada or the US later — always confirm current WES acceptance for your exact program before relying on this for an international plan.</p>
